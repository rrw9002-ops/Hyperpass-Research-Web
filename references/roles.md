# Roles and ownership

Head Manager is the main agent, not a tenth spawned profile. It owns mandate,
causal framing, dynamic delegation, acceptance, follow-ups, synthesis and memory.
The nine profiles preserve the original TradingCodex specialist identities.

| Exact profile | Question it owns | Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fundamental-analyst | Business economics, filings, statements, accounting quality | `methods/fundamental-analyst.md` |
| technical-analyst | Price, volume, trend, volatility, liquidity, market structure | `methods/technical-analyst.md` |
| news-analyst | News, disclosures, event chronology and narrative shifts | `methods/news-analyst.md` |
| macro-analyst | Rates, FX, policy, commodities, cycles and transmission | `methods/macro-analyst.md` |
| instrument-analyst | ETF/index/options/crypto structure and instrument mechanics | `methods/instrument-analyst.md` |
| valuation-analyst | Method fit, DCF/comps/reverse DCF, scenarios and sensitivity | `methods/valuation-analyst.md` |
| portfolio-manager | Portfolio fit, concentration, sizing context and opportunity cost | `methods/portfolio-manager.md` |
| risk-manager | Independent downside, constraints, failure modes and risk readiness | `methods/risk-manager.md` |
| judgment-reviewer | Independent evidence-to-judgment adjudication | `methods/judgment-reviewer.md` |

Method paths are relative to this directory. None is a mandatory roster.
Portfolio fit is not risk approval; a risk review is not order authority.
Judgment Reviewer consumes accepted evidence and names a revision owner instead
of creating replacement primary research or silently changing the user's frame.
Use `dispatch.md` for profile selection or explicit canonical role binding.
Never substitute a generic brief or Head Manager for independent risk/judgment
work. Role names alone do not establish canonical binding or independence.
